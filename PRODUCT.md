# Papaguy

## Platform

A browser-based live-performance tool for a DJ, with phone support as a secondary target.

## Performance

A colorful feathered parrot fills the stage view against pure black. The model shows the chest, head and wings. Each arm drives its corresponding wing. Opening the mouth opens the beak. Show mode hides the setup interface and camera preview entirely.

## Stack

Three.js, MediaPipe Tasks Vision and vanilla TypeScript. See [requirements](PRD.md) and [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md).
